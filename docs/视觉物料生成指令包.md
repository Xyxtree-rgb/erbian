# 耳辩 · 视觉物料生成指令包

> 用途：交给 AI 生图工具（Midjourney / 即梦 / 可画 / Recraft / Figma AI）直接出图。
> 每段提示词都可**单独整段复制**，不需要你改任何参数。
> 最后一节是"给我的格式要求"——按那个命名和尺寸导出，我直接接进代码。

---

## 0. 先把话说清：错在哪

| 问题 | 现状 | 应该是什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **主视觉被画成钥匙孔** | 我用地图节点、穿越转场里的 logo，用了「椭圆 + 矩形」拼成的钥匙孔 | 你的主视觉是**花苞**（`erbian.svg`：一个大圆弧包着细长花蕊，层层叠叠将开未开） |
| **UI 太丑** | 渡口引子页：竖条纹纸 + 三个圆圈数字 + 黑块按钮，像说明书 | 柔光、留白、克制，字距拉开的东方感 |
| **地图太丑** | 蓝白色块直接拼，边界是硬边，像填色游戏 | 水墨长卷：墨分五色、宣纸肌理、路径留白 |

**核心美术纪律（所有物料统一遵守）**：
- 底色只有**墨色系**：靛青 `#3d4a4f`、墨青 `#5d6b70`、磨白 `#f4f2ed`、炭黑 `#1f2326`
- **唯一允许的高饱和**：印章红 `#b4553f`，且只出现在判词 / 落款 / 篙头
- 不用金色、不用渐变塑料感、不用立体投影、不用圆角卡片
- 东方感靠**留白和字距**，不靠纹样堆砌

## 1. 主视觉：花苞标记（最优先）

这一步必须先出，后面所有界面都要用它。

### 1.1 提示词 · 水墨单色版（用于开屏、转场、水印）

```
A single bud-shaped emblem, viewed straight on, vertically composed.
The form is a large soft petal arc enclosing a slender pointed pistil, layered like a
flower about to open — NOT a keyhole, NOT a lock, no rectangular stem, no circular hole.

Rendered in Chinese ink wash (shui mo): the outer petal edge is a confident single brush
stroke that thickens and thins like calligraphy; interior filled with soft ink bleed,
ink-black core fading to pale grey at the rim. Ink density varies naturally (five tones
of ink). Paper texture of raw xuan rice paper visible through the wash.

Centered, generous white space, no border, no shadow, no gradient sheen.
Monochrome: deep indigo-black #1f2326 on warm off-white #f4f2ed.
Style: restrained, literati painting, modern minimal logo mark, high negative space.
--ar 3:4 --style raw
```

**负面词（一并粘贴）**
```
keyhole, lock, padlock, rectangular stem, circle hole, gold, metallic, 3D bevel,
drop shadow, plastic gloss, gradient sheen, neon, flat vector icon, emoji, text, letters,
watermark, busy background, drop cap, symmetrical mechanical shape
```

### 1.2 提示词 · 极简矢量版（用于 favicon、小图标 16–32px）

```
Minimal vector logo mark: a vertical bud silhouette made of exactly two shapes —
one wide crescent petal hugging a narrow pointed leaf — created as a single continuous
line with variable stroke weight (thick at the base, tapering to a fine point at the top).
Solid ink #1f2326, flat, no fill detail, no outline box.
Reads clearly at 16 pixels. Centered, lots of breathing room.
--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6
```

### 1.3 提示词 · 花瓣展开动效关键帧（用于穿越转场）

生成 4 张静态帧，我做成序列动画：

```
Four sequential frames of the same ink bud opening. Same composition, same camera.

Frame 1: tightly closed bud, single outline, ink very wet, edges soft and bleeding.
Frame 2: outer petal separates slightly, a thin crescent of paper shows between petals,
         ink still wet.
Frame 3: petal opens to 60 degrees, pistil visible as a grey slender form,
         less ink bleed, edges more defined.
Frame 4: fully open, five ink tones clear, edges crisp, small seal-red dot at the
         flower's center (only saturated color in the image).

All frames: centered on warm off-white #f4f2ed, no shadow, monochrome ink except the
single seal-red accent in frame 4.
--ar 3:4 --style raw
```

## 2. 渡口引子页（01 / 02 / 03 那个界面）

### 2.1 整页背景图

```
Background for a mobile screen, portrait 9:16.

An extremely quiet Chinese ink landscape reduced to almost nothing: a single horizontal
sweep of pale ink wash at the lower third suggesting a river's surface, and above it
vast empty xuan paper. Three tiny ink dots float at different heights on the right,
like distant boats. Ink tones only: warm off-white #f4f2ed paper, pale grey wash,
one whisper of indigo #5d6b70.

No mountains, no trees, no buildings, no text, no frame, no vignette, no gradient sky.
Soft rice-paper fiber texture, slightly uneven, like an unprinted sheet.
90% of the image must be empty space at the top.
--ar 9:16 --style raw
```

### 2.2 三个序号（01 / 02 / 03）的样式

不要圆圈，不要描边徽章。用**印章式汉字**：

```
Three small vertical seal stamps in a column, Chinese ink style.
Each is a slim vertical rectangle, hand-carved seal-stone texture, slightly irregular
edges (not a perfect rectangle), stamped in muted vermilion #b4553f with uneven ink
coverage at the corners — like a real seal pressed once on rice paper.
Inside each: one Chinese numeral carved in relief, simple and slightly weathered.
Amount of red is minimal, like a signature on a painting, not a badge.
Transparent background, PNG. --ar 1:1 --style raw
```

> 说明：真用汉字「一 / 二 / 三」，比 01/02/03 更贴水墨。若你想保留阿拉伯数字，把提示词里的
> `Chinese numeral` 改成 `small Arabic numeral in thin serif`。

### 2.3 主按钮「上船」

不要纯黑方块。要**墨痕下压的实体按钮**：

```
A single UI button for a mobile app, isolated on transparent background.

Shape: a long soft-edged horizontal bar, its outline made of a slightly uneven ink stroke
(hand-brushed, not a perfect rectangle). Fill: solid deep indigo #2b3438.
Inside the bar, at the right end, a thin ink chevron pointing right, drawn like a brush flick.
Height is short relative to width (about 1:4.5). Corners are softly rounded by 4px only —
it should read as a stone slab, not a pill.

No shadow below, no gloss, no gradient, no border outline, no glow.
--ar 4:1 --style raw
```

### 2.4 次按钮「回到选择」

```
A single secondary UI button, isolated on transparent background.
Just a thin hand-brushed ink underline beneath empty space — no box, no fill, no border.
The underline is uneven in thickness, fading at both ends like a dry brush stroke.
Ink #5d6b70. Above it is pure empty space where text will be placed.
--ar 4:1 --style raw
```

## 3. 世界观地图（渡口航线图）

这是最需要重做的一张。现在的蓝白块等于"填色游戏"，要的是**水墨长卷**。

### 3.1 主地图底图

```
A vertical Chinese ink-wash scroll painting, portrait orientation, bird's-eye view of a
river valley seen from directly above.

A single river winds from the top of the frame to the bottom in three gentle S-curves,
taking up the middle third of the width. The river is painted with negative space —
unpainted warm paper #f4f2ed left blank, outlined only where a faint ink wash touches
the far bank. No solid blue fill. A few short horizontal brush ticks mark the current.

Left and right banks: three layers of distant hills in progressively paler ink
(nearest dark #3d4a4f, middle 40% opacity, farthest 12% opacity), painted with broad
wet-on-wet strokes that dissolve at the edges — never outlined, never filled flat.

Small ink dots and thin strokes imply villages and bare trees along the banks,
extremely sparse, at most a dozen marks in the whole image.

The lowest fifth fades into fog: the ink simply stops, paper takes over.

Composition: a long vertical scroll, generous empty paper at top and bottom,
ink only in the middle band.
Monochrome ink with a single tiny vermilion seal accent near the bottom right.
No border, no title, no text, no grid, no icons, no roads.
--ar 9:16 --style raw
```

### 3.2 渡口节点（花苞造型，替换掉钥匙孔）

```
A tiny waypoint marker for a map, isolated on transparent background, PNG.
Shape: a minimal ink bud — one soft crescent petal cupping a single pointed pistil,
about as tall as it is wide, no stem, no base, no circle around it.
Drawn in a single continuous ink stroke with variable weight, tapering at the top.
Solid ink #2b3438, flat, no fill detail.
Small enough to read at 24 pixels. Slight irregularity is welcome — it must look
hand-painted, not geometric.
No keyhole, no lock, no pin, no drop shadow, no ring.
--ar 1:1 --style raw
```

### 3.3 已到访 / 未到访 两种状态

```
Same ink bud marker in two states, side by side, transparent background.

Left: unvisited. Outline only — a single dry brush stroke, incomplete in places,
pale grey #a8adae, as if lightly sketched.
Right: visited. Solid deep ink #2b3438 with a tiny vermilion #b4553f dot at its center.

Identical size and composition. No glow, no badge, no shadow, no text.
--ar 2:1 --style raw
```

### 3.4 水面质感（可选，作为地图叠加层）

```
A seamless texture tile of gentle water surface, Chinese ink style.
Only horizontal broken brush lines of varying length and pressure, pale grey on
transparent background. Sparse — large gaps of nothing between strokes.
No blue, no whitecaps, no reflections, no waves drawn as curves.
Must tile seamlessly left to right.
--ar 1:1 --style raw
```

## 4. 加载动画（替换掉现在那个丑的）

### 4.1 三帧循环（我做成 CSS 动画）

```
Three sequential frames of a single ink dot blooming into a bud, identical composition,
identical camera, transparent background.

Frame 1: one small round ink dot, wet, edges bleeding slightly into paper.
Frame 2: the dot elongates upward into a narrow tear shape, still wet, top edge soft.
Frame 3: the tear resolves into an open bud outline — a crescent petal and a pointed
         pistil — edges now defined, ink dry.

Monochrome #2b3438, no color accent, no shadow, no glow, centered, lots of empty space.
--ar 1:1 --style raw
```

### 4.2 备选：一笔画圆（更抽象的"等待"感）

```
A single continuous ink brush stroke that starts as a dot, sweeps into an incomplete
circle, and trails off into a dry brush tail. Begins thick and wet, ends thin and
scratchy. Monochrome ink #2b3438 on transparent background.
The circle is intentionally broken — about 40 degrees missing at the top right.
Composition centered, isolated. No arrow, no spinner, no gradient.
--ar 1:1 --style raw
```

## 5. 我要的文件格式（重要，按这个给我）

### 5.1 命名规则

放在一个文件夹里，**文件名照抄，别改**，我按名字自动替换：

```
erbian-bud-ink.png         主视觉·水墨单色版
erbian-bud-flat.png        主视觉·极简矢量版
erbian-bud-f1.png          花苞展开 第1帧
erbian-bud-f2.png          花苞展开 第2帧
erbian-bud-f3.png          花苞展开 第3帧
erbian-bud-f4.png          花苞展开 第4帧
intro-bg.png               引子页背景
seal-01.png                序号印章 一
seal-02.png                序号印章 二
seal-03.png                序号印章 三
btn-primary.png            主按钮「上船」
btn-secondary.png          次按钮「回到选择」
atlas-map.png              世界观地图底图
atlas-node.png             渡口节点·未到访
atlas-node-done.png        渡口节点·已到访
water-tile.png             水面纹理
loading-f1.png             加载动画 第1帧
loading-f2.png             加载动画 第2帧
loading-f3.png             加载动画 第3帧
```

### 5.2 尺寸与格式

| 文件 | 尺寸 | 格式 | 透明底 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `erbian-bud-ink.png` | 1600 × 2133（3:4） | PNG | **需要** |
| `erbian-bud-flat.png` | 1024 × 1024 | PNG | **需要** |
| `erbian-bud-f1~f4.png` | 各 1200 × 1600 | PNG | **需要** |
| `intro-bg.png` | 1080 × 1920（9:16） | PNG 或 JPG | 不需要 |
| `seal-01~03.png` | 各 512 × 512 | PNG | **需要** |
| `btn-primary.png` | 1400 × 320 | PNG | **需要** |
| `btn-secondary.png` | 1400 × 320 | PNG | **需要** |
| `atlas-map.png` | 1600 × 2844（9:16，长卷） | PNG | 不需要 |
| `atlas-node.png` / `-done.png` | 各 256 × 256 | PNG | **需要** |
| `water-tile.png` | 1024 × 1024 无缝 | PNG | **需要** |
| `loading-f1~f3.png` | 各 512 × 512 | PNG | **需要** |

**统一要求**
- 色彩空间：sRGB
- 不要预压缩到糊（PNG 无损，不要转成低质量 JPG）
- 透明底的图，**边缘要干净**，不要留白边或黑边
- 背景类图片不要带任何文字（文案我代码里写，方便改）

### 5.3 如果你的工具出不了透明底

那就**用纯白 `#ffffff` 作背景**，并在文件名后加 `-w`，例如 `erbian-bud-ink-w.png`。
我可以自己抠，但边缘会有半像素白边，所以能出透明底最好。

## 附：如果你只有一个通用生图工具

把下面这段当"全局前缀"，每次生成时加在提示词最前面：

```
Consistent art direction: Chinese ink wash (shui mo) on raw xuan rice paper.
Monochrome palette only — deep ink #1f2326, indigo #3d4a4f, warm off-white paper
#f4f2ed. The ONLY saturated color permitted is seal vermilion #b4553f, used sparingly.
No gold, no metallic sheen, no 3D bevel, no plastic gloss, no drop shadow.
Express the East through negative space and brush pressure, not through ornament.
The subject is always a FLOWER BUD (a petal cupping a pointed pistil) — never a keyhole.
```
